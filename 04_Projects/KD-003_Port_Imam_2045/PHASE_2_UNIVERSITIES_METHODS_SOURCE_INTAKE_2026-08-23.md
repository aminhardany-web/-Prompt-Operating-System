## Methodological correction required by the evidence protocol

The statement “leading ports in 2026 no longer use SWOT or Balanced Scorecard” is treated as an unverified universal claim and must not be promoted into the KD-003 baseline without direct comparative evidence.

SWOT, PESTEL and BSC may remain supporting diagnostic or performance-management tools. The strategic model should not assume that any one of them is the complete methodology.

Likewise, labels such as “Port Reform Toolkit Strategic Change Framework”, “IAPH Smart Port Framework” or “the exact method used by a named port” require source-specific verification before being used as formal framework names.

## Evidence classification rule

Every extracted statement must be classified as one of:

FACT — directly supported by a primary/current authoritative source.

INFERENCE — analytical synthesis derived from verified sources.

HYPOTHESIS — proposition requiring further testing.

ANALYTICAL OPINION — expert judgement, not represented as institutional fact.

UNVERIFIED — source lead retained for later verification.
