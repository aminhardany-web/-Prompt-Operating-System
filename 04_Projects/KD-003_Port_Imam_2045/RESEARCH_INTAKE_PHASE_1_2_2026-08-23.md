## Verified core

- World Bank Port Reform Toolkit, Third Edition (2025): official guide for port reform and improvement; addresses changing macroeconomic dynamics, supply-chain resilience, climate change, corporate responsibility, digitalisation, trend identification, structured change management, balancing efficiency/equity, risk sharing and sustainable development.
- UNCTAD TrainForTrade Port Management Programme: 240 hours over two years, eight modules and a final dissertation; content is adapted to local contexts and the dissertation addresses a real port problem with concrete management solutions.
- UNCTAD Port Management Series Volume 14: published 26 February 2026; case studies from the 2019–2024 English-speaking network.
- OECD: “agile, adaptable and aligned supply chains” is a supply-chain resilience concept, not a standalone port-strategy methodology.
- IAPH PRL-MF: structured readiness tool for marine-fuel transition; not a universal strategic-planning framework.
- Rotterdam: official digital evidence supports data sharing, standardized APIs, corridor collaboration, AI-supported decision making and operational testing.
- Antwerp-Bruges: official Smart Port model links economy, climate and people and documents digital twin, sensors, autonomous drones, smart cameras and ecosystem collaboration.
- Hamburg: SmartPORT and ChainPORT are verified; ChainPORT is an inter-port learning/benchmarking network, with the original formation source dating to 2016.
- Singapore MPA: official Maritime Digitalisation Playbook documents a Digital Transformation Framework organised around why/what/how and foundations including people, data/systems and partner ecosystem.
- Los Angeles: Clean Air Action Plan and zero-emission transition are official initiatives.
- Erasmus MEL: verified as a multidisciplinary maritime programme integrating port/shipping economics, logistics/supply chains and related management domains; exact 2026 required reading lists require course-level verification.
- NTU MSc Maritime Studies: verified maritime economics, shipping management, port management, maritime logistics, environmental shipping and sustainability/digitalisation domains.
- WMU remains a priority institutional reference; course-level 2026 reading claims require direct official evidence.
